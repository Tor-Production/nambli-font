Worked letter bodies and other non-deferred contours
10,536 processed or partly processed glyph/face pairs were inspected.
Before 1.001 -> candidate 1.002, using the same main-audit criteria:
  Endpoint curve tangent-break flags:       1,141 -> 0
  Short-handle corner flags:                7,386 -> 0
  Short straight facets between curves:      652 -> 0
  Proper crossing flags:                      1 -> 0
  Zero-length segment flags:                  1 -> 0
The complete candidate family has no detected proper crossings, zero-length
segments, open/disconnected contours, non-finite coordinates, or zero-area
contours under the frozen scanner. Filled contours and optical design still
require the owner's visual review; this is not a proof of perceptual perfection.

Deferred diacritics
The owner's pause on diacritic redesign is respected. Independently comparing
the actual compiled outlines verified 1,440 whole glyphs and 7,296 retained
accent contours exactly unchanged from 1.001, with zero preservation errors.
The remaining 57 tangent-break, 4,067 short-handle, and 102 short-facet flags
all belong to these unchanged deferred scopes. They must not be presented as
newly repaired. The review page's pending section exposes deferred glyphs.

Review-only curvature diagnostics
Worked-body radius-step alerts: 35,332 -> 31,582.
Worked-body short curvature-reversal alerts: 5,074 -> 8,186.
These screens are sensitive to quadratic segmentation and include natural
fillets and S-curves. They nominate review locations; they are not defect counts.
Line/line corners are separately classified as intentional-design review.

A supplemental exact interior-quadratic screen found 28 microscopic collinear
retraces in worked candidate bodies versus 5 in the baseline. Their excursions
past the nearest endpoint are 0.071–1.0 font unit. Two additional retraces are
in unchanged deferred glyphs in both versions. These are transparent microscopic
review findings, not established visible artifacts. The supplemental screen does
not change the frozen audit's criteria or counts; see microscopic-review-markers.json
and microscopic-review-markers-baseline.json.

Visual spot-check
Raster comparisons at 640-pixel em were inspected for e, r, k, s in Regular,
Light, and ExtraBoldItalic. Small facets and cap edges appeared smoother, with
no new visible bumps in those probes. Final-build raster files are saved as
rasters/focus-raster-Regular-reviewed.png, rasters/focus-raster-Light-reviewed.png, and
rasters/focus-raster-ExtraBoldItalic-reviewed.png. This spot-check does not mean every
glyph was individually visually approved. The interactive before/after review
is the owner's next approval step.
