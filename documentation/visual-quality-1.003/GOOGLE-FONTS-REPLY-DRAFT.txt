Hi Simon, thank you for the specific feedback and examples.

We have smoothed the outline joins and rounded terminals across the family,
and made .notdef distinct from O. I am keeping those improvements.

I tested added overshoot at the baseline and x-height, then chose to retain
the original alignment and round proportions. That alignment is part of the
appearance I want for Nambli; the added extensions changed its balance in a
way I did not want to keep. Candidate 1.003 therefore uses the original body
design with the contour smoothing retained. This is a design choice I would
like to evaluate through the proofs, rather than a claim that overshoot is
unnecessary in general.

For diacritics, I am making selective, modest weight adjustments where marks
are visibly weak, starting with the side caron in Ľ and related forms, rather
than thickening every accent. I want their weight, compact proportions, and
rounded construction to remain coherent with the letters.

I have prepared comparisons of the original, overshoot experiment, and revised
candidate at display and text sizes. Could you assess whether this direction
works for Nambli, and point out any letters or weights where the original
alignment remains a problem? If retaining it would prevent inclusion in
Google Fonts, I would appreciate knowing that before making further changes.

I understand that passing the automated checks does not replace Google
Fonts' design review. My aim is to address the outline and mark issues while
preserving Nambli's intended appearance.

Thank you,
Yurii
