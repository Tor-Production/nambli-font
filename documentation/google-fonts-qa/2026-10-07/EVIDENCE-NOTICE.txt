Public evidence contains path-redacted copies of native reports. The original raw JSON/logs remain unmodified in the local run directories; their SHA-256 values are recorded in each redaction-manifest.json. Redaction only replaces machine paths with <QA>, <PREPARATION> and <HOME>. Gzip mtime is fixed to zero. These copies are not claimed to be byte-identical native output. Expand a gzip with Python gzip.decompress to inspect every subresult.

Nambli font data and outline specimens remain under SIL OFL 1.1. The three new article JPEGs have their separately stated CC BY-SA 4.0 license. No upstream issue/PR or designer form was submitted from these drafts.

Large machine manifests, browser per-text outcomes, native log copies and the standard NAM diff are gzip-compressed (mtime zero). The NAM patch format, native trailing spaces and original whitespace are preserved inside gzip. Expand before git apply or inspection. Text line endings in public copies may differ through Windows/Git serialization; original raw-file SHA-256 values identify the untouched native artifacts.
