Google Fonts preparation — 2026-10-07

This is a public development release, not an accepted Google Fonts family.
The current 0.704 fonts retain the approved 872-character repertoire.

Fontspector 1.8.0 was rerun on all 12 licensed 0.704 TTFs with its
googlefonts profile: 1047 PASS, 218 WARN, 49 FAIL, 716 SKIP and 50 INFO.
There were no ERROR or FATAL results. Network checks were skipped.
These are subresult counts; some checks return multiple messages. The complete
grouped public report is fontspector-summary.json in this directory.

License, author metadata, RIBBI names and OS/2 codepage ranges were corrected.
Remaining FAIL groups are:
- 24 version_format messages: release 0.704 is below the required 1.000.
- 12 glyph_coverage messages: 20 required Latin Core characters are missing.
- 12 shape_languages messages: repertoire/shaping findings need review.
- 1 dirname_matches_nameid_1 message: the current distribution layout uses
  a ttf directory, whereas Google's repository check expects nambli.

The shape_languages check heuristically infers GF glyphsets and tests their
languages. This is different from the earlier 238-locale CLDR 48.2 exemplar
baseline. Its FAIL messages mention 47 GF language identifiers; direct base-code
matches with that baseline are teo_Latn (Teso) and yo_Latn (Yoruba). Teso needs
review of seven modifier letters; Yoruba needs review of U+0329 and related
sequences. WARN messages mention 24 identifiers, including direct matches for
Igbo, Luo, Nuer, Riffian and Yoruba. Base-code matching is not an exhaustive
mapping: dialects, aliases and macrolanguages can create additional overlap.
Many inferred tests extend beyond the documented CLDR baseline, but actual
overlaps remain. These findings are pending review, not automatically dismissed
and not a claim that all flagged languages are wholly unsupported.

Twenty Latin Core additions and four unencoded source alternatives have been
prepared separately for all twelve faces in TTF, OTF and WOFF2. Those candidates
have 892 characters and 911 glyphs, with all prior 887 outlines/metrics verified
unchanged. They are NOT included in release 0.704. New designs need visual
approval, and locale activation of the unencoded alternatives needs work.

Outstanding work includes integrating reviewed Latin Core additions, resolving
the shaping findings, reviewing overlap/soft-dotted warnings, a fully verified
production rebuild, and the contributor's Google CLA. The CLA has not been
signed. Editable UFO3 snapshots
are included and their original 0.7.3 geometry/feature round trips were verified.
Public license and metadata in those snapshots have been updated to match.

The project uses AI tools; this must be disclosed in the submission.
Google's curatorial acceptance has not been requested or granted yet.
