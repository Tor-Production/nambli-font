DESIGN REFINEMENT
All twelve styles contain 970 encoded characters and 998 glyphs. Compared with
the previous candidate (671f777), 21 forms per style changed: @; P/p with hook;
T/t with hook; T/r/R retroflex tails; g/h/H/W/w with hook; reversed ezh;
crossed-tail j, dotless j and J; Latin beta capitals/lowercase; i-ogonek and
its dotless alternate. Review all 252 cards in:
  refinement/Nambli-Refinement-Review.html
The complete comparison with public v0.7.4 remains in:
  review/Nambli-Technical-Review.html

@ uses the approved a at uniform 0.64 scale. Its circular bowl is now centered
on the outer loop, with a curvature-continuous right return. Saved-font center
error is at most 0.2881 font units; the pre-quantization a-counter XOR is zero.
U+01B9 reuses the existing U+025B outline, retaining its distinct Unicode mapping.
The 36 crossed-tail counters deviate from circles by at most 0.867 units after
TTF rounding. Hook joins are continuous; selected italic backtracking segments
found by final QA were repaired locally. TOP mark anchors follow the revised
stems and heights; other anchor classes and unrelated layout remain unchanged.

PRESERVATION
All 872 release Unicode mappings and 887 release metrics per face remain.
10,612 of 10,644 release glyph instances retain exact normalized geometry.
The 32 exceptions are @ and i-ogonek in all twelve styles, plus heavy ɬ/Ɬ
aperture repairs in four styles. The refinement changed 21 of 998 forms per

See approved-outline-preservation.json and refinement-integration.json.

PRODUCTION BUILD
