TECHNICAL CANDIDATE

All twelve styles contain 970 encoded characters and 998 glyphs. Compared with
the 872-character public release, 20 Latin Core characters and 78 further
characters were added. The latter close observed language-repertoire gaps and
include two invisible separators and a dotted circle. Extra unencoded forms
support localized carons, middle dots, Turkic i and soft-dotted behavior.
The source does not import outlines from another typeface.

The @ now derives its inner bowl from the existing a at uniform 0.64 scale,
with a circular outer loop and a connected return. Two italic terminal joins
were trimmed while retaining the exact inner counters. Collapsed or very small
apertures in ɬ / Ɬ were reopened in Bold and ExtraBold, upright and italic.

All 872 previous Unicode mappings and all 887 previous glyph metrics are
retained in every style. Of 10,644 original glyph instances, 10,624 retain
exact normalized geometry; the 20 visible exceptions are @ in all twelve
styles and the two aperture repairs in four styles. Exact redundant outline
points were also removed without changing their geometric paths.
See approved-outline-preservation.json and the before/after review page.

PRODUCTION BUILD

The twelve editable UFO3 masters compile independently through Fontmake 3.12.1
and ufo2ft 3.9.1. Reference binaries are optional validation inputs, not build
dependencies. Features, kerning and STAT are compiled from editable data.

The final TTF build passed 11,976 exact quadratic-outline comparisons and
105,696 HarfBuzz shaping comparisons, with unchanged candidate metrics, cmap,
glyph order and compiled layout. A second build produced identical SHA-256
hashes for all twelve TTFs and twelve WOFF2s.
See production-build-validation.json and sources/BUILDING.md.

The twelve supplementary OTFs carry the same design repairs and language
layout. Their preservation checks pass, but they are not outputs of the
independent UFO-to-TTF/WOFF2 build. See supplementary-otf-validation.json.
Do not claim that the current one-command build reproduces OTF.

REMAINING REVIEW

136 WARN subresults: 48 decomposed side-caron manual-inspection notices;
12 each for GF subset serving coverage, unregistered vendor ID NAMB, vertical
metrics, proportional math widths, missing rupee and legacy empty .null;
7 approximate collinearity, 5 jaggy-segment and 4 overlap notices.

Remaining tiny quantized-segment warnings are not asserted to be perfect
outlines. They include a roughly one-unit reversal in SemiBoldItalic @ and
other small joins. Existing exact geometry was not changed merely to suppress
approximate heuristics. See WARNING-DISPOSITIONS.txt and the full messages in
fontspector-summary.json; these findings remain available for visual review.

The 1.6 UPM line-metric span retains space for existing tall accents and deep
descenders. Math signs remain proportionally spaced. NAMB is not claimed as
a registered Microsoft vendor ID. Extra script labels have not been added to
hide Google subset-coverage warnings. The rupee sign is not in this candidate.

Visual approval of the new/changed forms, native-reader review where needed,
Google's curatorial decision, serving metadata and remaining contributor
checklist confirmations are still pending. A zero-FAIL report does not replace
these reviews or establish copyright ownership. AI assistance remains disclosed
in PROVENANCE.txt and the submission.
