Yurii Tor provided the creative direction for Nambli, a rounded display typeface with extended Latin and Cyrillic coverage and particular attention to Ukrainian. He reviewed specimens and directed corrections to the letter construction, joins, tails, proportions, stroke weights and spacing. Nambli’s development includes disclosed AI-assisted outline programming and font engineering. Its editable sources and build instructions are published under the SIL Open Font License.
