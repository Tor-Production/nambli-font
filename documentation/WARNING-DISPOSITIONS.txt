JUSTIFIED_TRADE_OFF / onboarding decision. Non-RIBBI legacy family names use
weight-specific name ID 1 values while preferred family IDs identify Nambli.
The 16 messages cover two platform records for each of eight non-RIBBI faces.
Current OpenType guidance recommends this four-style legacy grouping together
with name IDs 16/17 for an extended family. The package also passes the
OpenType family naming, maximum-four-faces, style-bit and preferred-family
consistency checks. Changing these records is a compatibility decision;
metadata continues to declare all 12 styles with correct weights/italic status.

outline_colinear_vectors: 6 WARN
JUSTIFIED_TRADE_OFF / visual decision if further editing is desired. The check
uses a 0.1-radian tolerance between consecutive straight segments. The six
WARN subresults contain 47 approximately collinear joins and zero exactly
collinear triples. Removing each middle point would alter the outline by
0.006564 to 1.238336 font units at that point; no lossless deletion was found.
Exact zero/redundant-point cleanup from the prior release is retained.
Individual messages/coordinates remain in the raw report. These were not
globally removed or waived by severity changes. A further simplification
would require a separately validated outline candidate.

outline_jaggy_segments: 4 package/binary WARN in approved 1.000
PATCH_VERIFIED_LOCALLY + NEEDS_OWNER_INPUT. The separate 1.001 candidate removes
the guarded very short backtracking segments in Bold/BoldItalic U+A7C8; those
two warnings disappear. It also removes a short reverse segment in Medium
U+1D7D, but the sharp bar/counter junction remains a heuristic warning. LightItalic
U+00B6 has two sharp stem/bowl joins within one WARN. Those two remaining WARN
require a more visible fillet/shape decision; they are not called fixed. All
eight changed contour pairs are shown before/after. Production 1.000 is unchanged.

overlapping_path_segments: 4 package/binary WARN in approved 1.000
PATCH_VERIFIED_LOCALLY + NEEDS_OWNER_INPUT for adopting the versioned candidate.
Four tiny rounded intersections affected MediumItalic/SemiBold/SemiBoldItalic
U+02A3 and Bold U+02A6. Selected paths were unioned with winding preserved;
two precisely guarded one-unit excursions introduced by quantization were
removed. All four overlap warnings disappear in the final 1.001 candidate.
All other glyphs, metrics, cmap, layout and kerning remain unchanged. The
candidate is not included in the Google submission or published release.

JUSTIFIED_TRADE_OFF / optional modern-font cleanup. The legacy unencoded .null
