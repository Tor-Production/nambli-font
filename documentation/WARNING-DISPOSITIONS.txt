Nambli candidate QA dispositions — 2026-10-07

These notes are evidence and decisions to review, not blanket check waivers.
The final family must be checked after all candidate patches and Fontmake export.

FIXED / IMPLEMENTED

1. Zero outline segments and exact collinear redundant line points.
   clean_outlines.py removes only exact local degeneracies. All 36 baseline
   TTF/OTF/WOFF2 files passed post-save exact normalized-segment equality,
   cmap/order/metrics equality and unrelated-table byte equality. Cubic outlines
   needed no cleanup. Reports list each removed native point and its reason.

2. Collapsed counters in uni026C (ɬ) / uniA7AD (Ɬ).
   The original ExtraBoldItalic has no counter in either TTF or OTF. ExtraBold's
   aperture was ~3 square font units; Bold's was also very small. The candidate
   reuses the matching Medium aperture in Bold and ExtraBold, upright and italic,
   translating it with the stem thickness. All existing outside contours and
   spacing remain exact. This is a visible correction requiring designer review.

3. @, U+0040.
   The original fixed outside bowl radius caused a bulge beyond the right stem
   at lighter weights. After rejecting an oval candidate, the current candidate
   reuses the ACTUAL approved a outline at a uniform 0.64 scale. This preserves
   its circular bowl/counter and optical stroke proportions; it is not redrawn
   as an arbitrary ellipse. The outer loop is also circular. The return is one
   cubic with vertical endpoint tangents, matching stem and circular outer loop.
   In LightItalic and Italic only, the obsolete a exit beyond native x=574 is
   trimmed where the continuing return takes over. The counter remains exactly
   the same geometric segments in both TTF and OTF; the other ten faces are
   unchanged by this terminal correction.
   Original advance/sidebearing remain; all other glyph outlines remain exact.
   Before/after: circular-at-all12-before-after.png. Early reference proof:
   circular-at-Regular-Bold-proof.png. Older at-before-after.png is superseded.

4. Soft dots and localized alternates.
   layout_augment.py adds separate dotless alternatives for all supported relevant
   Soft_Dotted characters; original glyphs remain. Contextual substitution ignores
   intervening below marks. Catalan dots and Turkic i are wired into locl. A side
   caron reuses the original precomposed dcaron contour; its alternate is positioned
   by the language helper. Final HarfBuzz and Fontspector checks are required.

REMAINING CHECKS THAT NEED A REASONED DISPOSITION

outline_colinear_vectors:
  The checker uses approximate collinearity. Exact cleanup can reveal MORE such
  warnings by joining identical straight pieces into longer lines, even though
  every geometric segment is proved unchanged. Example BoldItalic B:
  (12,69)->(13,80)->(116,660), integer cross product -553, is not exactly collinear.
  Do not delete these points under the exact cleanup policy. Candidate changes
  would require an explicit geometric tolerance and separate visual review.

overlapping_path_segments:
  Exact duplicated zero segments are removed. Some warnings concern sub-unit
  quadratic reversals / rounded comparison coordinates, e.g. Bold uni02A6
  (391,69.5),(391,70),(391,69), and SemiBoldItalic uni02A3
  (464,77),(464,77),(464,77.5). These are not exactly zero curves and were retained.
  Quantization cleanup with a tolerance is a separate change, not an exact fix.

alt_caron:
  uni013D,uni010F,uni013E,uni0165 have decomposed outlines. The check says it cannot
  inspect components, not that the visible caron is wrong. Existing actual mark
  contours are separate and on the right. Verify the multilingual proof visually;
  do not restructure approved glyphs solely to make the component heuristic pass.

vertical_metrics:
  hhea ascender 1200 / descender -400 gives 1.6 UPM, outside the recommendation
  1.2–1.5. Original actual family extrema already reach about 1175.8 and -326,
  a 1501.8-unit span, before additional stacked marks. Keep room to avoid clipping.
  New repertoire may need more space; check final bounds and stacking. Reducing
  metrics merely to silence this recommendation is not justified.

math_signs_width:
  Existing < > ≤ ≥ are width590; most math signs width620. This is a proportional
  spacing decision. Aligning widths affects spacing; review deliberately instead
  of treating it as an invisible metadata fix.

vendor_id:
  NAMB is not in the checker registry. Registration with Microsoft is an external
  administrative action; do not claim it is registered or borrow another ID.

rupee:
  ₹ is not supplied in the approved repertoire. Adding it is a new visible glyph
  and needs design review; absence is not proof the covered Latin/Slavic alphabets
  are unusable. It remains a Google Fonts check recommendation until addressed.

unreachable_subsetting:
  Font cmap coverage and GF served subset coverage are separate. Review the exact
  final uncovered codepoints against current GF subset definitions. Do not add
  an unsupported script label merely to suppress the warning; retain useful
  direct-download glyphs and discuss the serving boundary with Google Fonts.

unreachable_glyphs:
  .null is a legacy unmapped empty glyph. Removing it changes glyph order and is
  unnecessary for rendering. The four regional alternatives should become reachable
  after locl wiring; verify that no other substantive alternate remains unreachable.

HINTING
  The baseline is unhinted; there is no hinting FAIL. gasp exists, fpgm/prep/cvt do
  not. Do not fabricate ttfautohint version metadata. Autohinting is optional new
  rasterization behavior and requires small-size Windows/browser visual checks.

CHECKS OWNED BY OTHER CANDIDATE STAGES
  Coverage/shaping, separators, dotted circle, script-language meta and WWS flag
  are handled by core/language/metadata stages. Verify the final reports rather
  than carrying forward their baseline status.
